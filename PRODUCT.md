## Users

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Asmaa Wafik, a UI/UX designer, presenting case studies and highlight work to win interviews and job opportunities. Success is a hiring manager or recruiter coming away confident in her design judgment and contacting her (email or LinkedIn).

## Positioning

Positioned as a junior-to-mid-level product/UX designer who works inside product teams on real, specific flows — not a generalist portfolio of visual exercises. The differentiator is range plus rigor: enterprise multi-module ERP workflows, healthcare booking, a two-sided marketplace, and POS/retail systems, each framed around a concrete usability problem and the reasoning behind the decision, not just polished final screens. Explicitly favors "honest, well-reasoned design decisions over polished-looking claims" (existing About copy).

## Operating Context

Case studies cover three main projects (BCare, Fooj, Self-Order Kiosk) plus lighter "Highlights" pieces — including two mini case studies with their own dedicated pages (Ored POS, Microtec ERP) alongside gallery-style highlights (van sales app, Ogreen POS before/after, Microtec company website redesign). Work spans mobile apps, enterprise web systems, web/tablet POS, and a touch-first kiosk. Contributions were typically as part of a product team on specific screens/flows/features, with BCare and the Microtec website redesign called out as more end-to-end/solo ownership; the Self-Order Kiosk case study is framed around UI/UX design plus a research- and design-system-driven contribution (identifying a value-token gap and helping build it). Microtec ERP is deliberately kept as a lighter mini case study rather than a full case study, since the contribution there was broad but distributed across many modules rather than end-to-end ownership of one flow.
